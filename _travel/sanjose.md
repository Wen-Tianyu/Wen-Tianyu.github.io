## 📍 Food & Places

<div class="place-card" markdown="1">
**Shang Cafe San Jose (香小馆)**  
<a class="place-addr" href="https://maps.google.com/?q=6154+Bollinger+Rd,+San+Jose,+CA+95129">6154 Bollinger Rd, San Jose, CA 95129</a>

The best Chinese restaurant in San Jose area with authentic taste. If you like Szechuan cuisines, never miss this one!
</div>

<div class="place-card" markdown="1">
**Ping's Bistro Cupertino (留湘小聚)**  
<a class="place-addr" href="https://maps.google.com/?q=10700+S+De+Anza+Blvd,+Cupertino,+CA+95014">10700 S De Anza Blvd, Cupertino, CA 95014</a>

Also a wonderful Chinese restaurant with signatures of Yunnan cuisine. Before visiting there, I would never imagine how tasty mushrooms can be cooked.
</div>

<div class="place-card" markdown="1">
**Lei Garden (利苑)**  
<a class="place-addr" href="https://maps.google.com/?q=10125+Bandley+Dr,+Cupertino,+CA+95014">10125 Bandley Dr, Cupertino, CA 95014</a>

There are several Hong Kong style morning tea restaurant in Bay Area and this one makes the perfect taste with elegant vibe for dining in.
</div>

<div class="place-card" markdown="1">
**3Catea (三喵制茶)**  
<a class="place-addr" href="https://maps.google.com/?q=1777+N+Milpitas+Blvd+Milpitas,+CA+95035">1777 N Milpitas Blvd Milpitas, CA 95035</a>

Mochi is all I need and also all the reason for my love towards there.
</div>

<div class="place-card" markdown="1">
**Chicha San Chen (吃茶三千)**  
<a class="place-addr" href="https://maps.google.com/?q=20688+Stevens+Creek+Blvd,+Cupertino,+CA+95014">20688 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino, CA 95014</a>

The most popular boba shop in Bay Area and if you want to have a taste, remember to order ahead online to avoid a huge crowd in store. Fruit tea there is the best!
</div>
