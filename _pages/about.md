I am a **Machine Learning Engineer** at <a class="tag-tiktok" href="https://www.tiktok.com/about?lang=en">TikTok</a>, working on e-commerce recommendation. Before that, I was a graph learning researcher, studying Data Science at <span class="tag-pku">Peking University</span> and Electrical & Computer Engineering at <span class="tag-uiuc">UIUC</span>.

I am fluent in Python and C++ and work with PyTorch and TensorFlow. I enjoy learning the mathematics behind AI algorithms, and I want to apply it in more fields, including Computer Vision and Data Mining.
